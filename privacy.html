Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or book a stay, why we use it, and your rights. We are Longscroft Manor Ltd, [registered address], the controller of your information under UK data protection law. Contact us at Admin@longscroftmanor.com.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, email address, phone number (if given), your dates and number of guests.
  • Payment details: your card or wallet payment is handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment and limited card information (such as the last four digits), never your full card number.
  • Marketing preference: whether you asked to hear about offers.
  • Messages to our guest assistant: the questions you type into the "Ask a question" chat.
  • Technical information: your IP address and basic request data, kept in server logs for security and to prevent abuse.

How we use it, and why

  • To take and manage your booking, take payment, and send you confirmation and arrival information (to perform our contract with you).
  • To answer your questions through the guest assistant (our legitimate interest in helping visitors).
  • To keep the website secure and prevent fraud (our legitimate interest, and legal obligations).
  • To send you occasional offers and news, only if you ticked the box at checkout (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us).
  • To keep financial records (legal obligation).

Where it's kept, and who we share it with

Your booking and contact details are stored in our own booking database on servers run by Render (render.com) in [region], accessible only to us. We share the minimum needed with:

  • Stripe, our payment processor, which receives your payment details and our booking reference.
  • Guesty, the calendar system used by Rococo of Bath, which receives only your dates and booking reference, not your name or contact details, so the property's calendar shows the dates as booked.
  • Resend and Twilio, which deliver our emails and text messages to you.
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI behind the guest assistant. Your chat messages are sent to it to generate answers. Please don't include sensitive personal information in the chat.
  • Our website host, Render (render.com).
  • Google Fonts, which serves the website's typeface and receives your IP address when pages load.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK law, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or adequacy regulations. We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept for 6 years for accounting and legal purposes. If you start a booking but don't pay, your details are deleted after 7 days. "Pay on your phone" links and the details saved with them are deleted after 24 hours. Marketing preferences are kept until you unsubscribe. Server logs are kept for [30 days].

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy of it. Contact us at Admin@longscroftmanor.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
